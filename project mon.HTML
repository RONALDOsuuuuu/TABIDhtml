Some of the world's most famous monuments include:

Taj Mahal: A white marble mausoleum and a symbol of love.

Eiffel Tower: The iconic iron tower and symbol of France.

Great Wall of China: One of the longest man-made structures in the world.

Statue of Liberty: A symbol of freedom and democracy.

Pyramids of Giza: Ancient pyramids and the only surviving Wonder of the Ancient World.

Colosseum: A famous Roman amphitheater known for gladiator contests.

These monuments are renowned for their history, architecture, and cultural significance, and many are recognized as UNESCO World Heritage Sites.
